# Signal — product pitch

Signal is a market watchlist for people who track a lot of stocks but only check in occasionally. The problem isn't getting prices — it's that a normal watchlist is a wall of blinking numbers, and you have to scan every row to see what changed.

Signal does that scanning for you. It flags a stock only when its move is unusual for that specific stock — against its own recent behavior and the market — and only when a couple of signals line up, so you're not buried in noise. Every flag says why. When nothing matters, it says so.
